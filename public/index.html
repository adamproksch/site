What you get

    

      

        
Deploy on push

        
Push to your branch and the bot rebuilds. A broken build doesn't take it down, the last working version stays up.

      

      

        
Web console

        
Logs, restarts and a shell right in the browser.

      

      

        
Crash alerts

        
When the bot crashes we restart it and post the last 20 log lines to a channel of your choice.

      

      

        
Backups

        
Files and databases are backed up automatically. How long we keep them depends on your plan.

      

      

        
Pick a region

        
US East, US West, Frankfurt, London, Singapore or Sydney.

      

      

        
Isolated

        
Every bot gets its own container, so a noisy neighbour can't eat your CPU.
