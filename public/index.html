What you get

The boring parts of running a bot, handled, so you can get back to writing commands.

Git deploys

Connect a branch. Every push builds the project and restarts the bot, and a failed build leaves the running version alone.

Browser console

Live logs and a command line in the panel. No SSH key to set up or lose.

Crash recovery

A crashed bot restarts on its own and posts the last 20 log lines to a Discord channel you choose.

Backups

Files and databases are copied every day. Restore any copy from the panel.

Six regions

US East, US West, EU West, EU Central, Singapore and Sydney. Pick the one closest to your members.

Isolation

Each bot gets its own container with fixed CPU and memory, so another customer's bot can't slow yours down.

From repo to online in three steps

  1. 01

    Connect your repo

    Link GitHub or upload a zip. BotNest detects the language and picks a start command you can edit.

  2. 02

    Add your token

    Paste the bot token and any environment variables. They are encrypted, and the panel never shows them again.

  3. 03

    Deploy

    Watch the build in the console. When the health check passes, the bot is online.
